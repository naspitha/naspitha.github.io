About Me

I am a scientist who has spent most of her graduate school time researching spectroscopy of solar-energy materials, before finding herself increasingly passionate about chemistry education research. An Alexander-von-Humboldt postdoctoral fellowship brought me from the US to Germany in 2022, where I spent some time at Tiemann Chemistry Education Lab developing and evaluating simulation-based activities for chemistry. In 2024, I joined the bologna.lab, an innovative teaching & learning team at HU-Berlin that supports instructors across all subjects at the university. This website was initially created to host my teaching portfolio as part of the Delta program at UW-Madison, but it is also a starting point linking to my research and other professional projects.
