Decision-making tool for hybrid teaching

This web-based tool helps university instructors decide if hybrid teaching (the simultaneous teaching in-person and online) is a fitting choice for their course and provides a variety of tips for teaching and planning in different formats. I have programmed this tool completely from scratch and the teaching and technical tips associated with it were developed by me and two other colleagues from the Berlin Hybrid Teaching Network .

SILC - Chemistry simulations

SILC stands for Simulationen für Interaktives Lernen in Chemie (Simulations for Interactive Learning in Chemistry)! It is a library of newly simulations and activities on topics across the early undergraduate chemistry curriculum. The activities were originally developed in German, in order to be used at the introductory chemistry course at the Humboldt University of Berlin. They are currently being translated into English.

Beer's Law Simulation
& Activity

This simulation was developed to give undergraduate analytical chemistry students a more intuitive explanation as to why Beer's law (i.e. absorbance being directly proportional to concentration) holds. I have co-authored two chemistry education publications investigating the effects of an activity based on this simulation on students' reasoning about the phenomenon of absorption.

KinetiKit

KinetiKit is a Python-based software package that I developed during my graduate work. It can be used to analyze temporal data (in my case, time-resolved photoluminescence of semiconductors) using models of any desired complexity that represent the physical behavior of particles in a system.
